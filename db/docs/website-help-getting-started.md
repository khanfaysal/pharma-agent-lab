# Getting Started

## 1. Your first search

Type a medicine name into the search bar. You can enter a brand name (*Napa*), a
generic name (*Paracetamol*), a manufacturer (*Square Pharmaceuticals*), or a
condition (*fever*). MedIndex figures out which one you meant and shows matching
groups.

If you get too many results, use the filter rail on the left: dosage form,
strength, manufacturer, therapeutic class, and price range.

## 2. Reading a brand record

A brand record shows:

- **Brand name** and manufacturer
- **Generic** — click through for the full clinical monograph
- **Form** — tablet, capsule, syrup, suspension, injection, suppository, and so on
- **Strength** — per unit or per 5 ml for liquids
- **Pack size** — what you buy
- **Price** — indicative maximum retail price for that pack

The **See all brands of this generic** button is the one most people want: it
lists every equivalent product sorted by price.

## 3. Reading a monograph

The generic page is the clinical record. Sections appear in this order:
indication, mode of action, adult dose, child dose, renal dose, administration,
contraindication, precaution, interaction, side effect, pregnancy category.

Blank sections mean the source did not supply the information — not that there is
nothing to report.

## 4. Comparing prices

Open any generic, then sort the brand list by price. Compare *per unit*, not per
pack: divide price by the pack quantity. The comparison table does this for you
where pack size is machine-readable.

## 5. Using the assistant

Click **Ask** in the top bar. Good questions look like:

- "Which company makes Seclo and what does it cost?"
- "What are the cheapest brands of Metformin 500mg?"
- "What is the renal dose for Ciprofloxacin?"
- "How long do you keep my search history?"
- "Which antihypertensives are in your database from Beximco?"

It answers from the database and from our published documents, and it cites both.
It will not tell you what to take.

## 6. Saving and exporting

Free accounts can save records to lists. Institutional accounts can export a
result set to CSV and access the REST API.

## 7. Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Focus search |
| `g` then `b` | Browse brands |
| `g` then `g` | Browse generics |
| `g` then `c` | Browse companies |
| `a` | Open the assistant |
| `?` | Show shortcuts |
