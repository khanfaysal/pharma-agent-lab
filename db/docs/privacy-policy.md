# Privacy Policy

MedIndex ("we", "us") operates a pharmaceutical reference service for healthcare
professionals and the general public. This policy explains what we collect, why
we collect it, and what control you have over it.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information
When you register we collect your name, email address, occupation (for example
Physician, Pharmacist, Student, or General User), professional registration
number where you supply one, and your district. Occupation and district are used
only to tailor which reference content is surfaced first; they are never sold.

### 1.2 Search and usage data
We record the search terms you submit, the medicine records you open, the
filters you apply, and coarse timing information. Search logs are retained for
18 months in identifiable form, then aggregated irreversibly.

### 1.3 Device and technical data
IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and approximate city-level
location derived from IP. We do not collect precise GPS location.

### 1.4 Information we do NOT collect
We do not collect prescriptions, patient records, diagnoses, or any personal
health information about you or anyone you treat. Do not paste patient
identifiers into the search box or the AI assistant. If you do, that text enters
our search logs and is subject to the retention periods above.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To operate search, filtering, and the AI assistant.
- To rank results — for example, showing formulations relevant to your stated
  specialty higher.
- To detect abuse, scraping, and automated bulk extraction of our database.
- To produce aggregate statistics (e.g. "most searched generics this month").
  These statistics are published only in a form that cannot identify any user.

We do not use your data to train third-party foundation models. Queries sent to
the AI assistant are transmitted to our model providers for inference only,
under contracts that prohibit training on our traffic.

## 3. Legal Bases

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: contract performance (operating your
account), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, service
improvement), and consent (marketing email, which is opt-in only and separately
revocable).

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure

We share data with:

- **Infrastructure providers** — cloud hosting and database services, bound by
  data-processing agreements.
- **AI model providers** — the text of your assistant queries, and the retrieved
  reference passages, for the duration of generating a response.
- **Legal authorities** — only on valid legal process, and we notify you unless
  legally prohibited.

We never sell personal data. We do not share individual-level data with
pharmaceutical manufacturers. Sponsored brand placements are sold on an
aggregate-impression basis only; a sponsor never learns who viewed their listing.

## 5. Data Retention

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Account record | Life of the account plus 30 days |
| Search logs (identifiable) | 18 months |
| Aggregated analytics | Indefinite |
| AI assistant transcripts | 90 days |
| Backups | 35 days rolling |

## 6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data by
emailing privacy@medindex.example. We respond within 30 days. Deletion removes
your account record and de-identifies your search history; it does not remove
aggregated statistics that have already been computed.

You may opt out of personalised ranking in Settings → Privacy without losing
access to any reference content.

## 7. Security

Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.2 or higher. Passwords are stored using
Argon2id. Database access is restricted to named engineers with individual
credentials and is logged. We run third-party penetration tests annually.

## 8. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## 9. Changes

Material changes are announced by email and in-product at least 14 days before
taking effect. The effective date at the top of this document always reflects
the current version.

Contact: privacy@medindex.example
