# About MedIndex

## What we do

MedIndex is a pharmaceutical reference database covering the Bangladesh market.
We index 28,000+ marketed brand packs across 2,500+ generic monographs from 670+
manufacturers, plus a separate herbal and traditional medicine section.

Our purpose is narrow and we keep it that way: make it fast to find what a
medicine is, who makes it, what it costs, and what its label says.

## Who it is for

- **Prescribers** checking a formulation, a renal adjustment, or an interaction.
- **Pharmacists** identifying an equivalent when a brand is out of stock.
- **Students** learning the therapeutic classification of a drug family.
- **Patients and carers** who want to understand something they were prescribed
  — with the standing caveat that we are a reference, not a clinician.

## Where the data comes from

Records are compiled from manufacturer product submissions, published package
inserts, and regulatory listings. We normalise names, deduplicate packs, link
generics to indications and therapeutic classes, and attach indicative maximum
retail prices as supplied.

We do not author clinical content. When a monograph field is blank, it is because
the source did not supply it — we do not fill gaps with inference.

## Editorial independence

Manufacturers can pay for sponsored placement. They cannot pay to:

- change the clinical content of a monograph,
- remove a competitor from results,
- alter price information,
- influence what the AI assistant says.

Sponsored listings carry a visible label everywhere they appear. If you ever see
sponsorship affecting clinical content, write to editorial@medindex.example and
we will publish the correction.

## Corrections

Every record has a **Report an issue** link. Corrections are triaged within five
working days. Price corrections are verified against the manufacturer before
publication. We keep a public changelog of monograph revisions.

## The assistant

In 2025 we added a retrieval-augmented AI assistant. It sits on top of the same
database you can browse — it has no private knowledge and cannot answer from
memory. Every claim it makes is traced to either a database row or a passage in
our published documents, and it shows you which.

We publish how it is built, including its retrieval parameters and its evaluation
scores, in the Product Documentation. We think a reference service that adds a
language model owes its users that transparency.
